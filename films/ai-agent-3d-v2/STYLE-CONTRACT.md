# Glass Signal 3D — custom direction
Original fictional AI core: a faceted processor inside metal and transmissive glass rings, with six light-guide satellite modules. The physical glass/metal/light-guide materials are learned from the official Glass Product Render code, while object geometry, story and staging are original.

FullHD 1080x1920, 24fps, 12s / 288 frames, 2× internal geometry supersampling, neutral tone mapping. Deep focus. Real camera and object transformations change reflections. Single ice-blue/violet light family on neutral materials. Authored visual captions in frosted bars, no fake speech/ASR.

This custom direction is NOT exact Glass Product Render parity: strip PMREM is baked once, there is no dynamic mirror, caustic floor, physical depth-of-field or bloom. Those costly native passes were benchmarked on software GL and rejected as unfeasible for this repair, instead of lowering output resolution/fps. The final image and performance are measured again. No claim that test passes establish artistic equivalence.

Critical text in x60..930/y210..1440. Brand y269, title y357, subtitle y437, caption panel y1290..1386. Hero close-up, diagonal exploded orbit, six-node network, assembly snap/end orbit. Silent AAC for playback compatibility; H.264 High level 4.1, square pixels, BT709 VUI and faststart.
