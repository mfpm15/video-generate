# Sample Seven

A 14-second scene, original characters. The starting example: read it whole before planning any project.

4 a.m. in a university chemistry lab. Mei, 24, a graduate student, has been watching one flask all night. Professor
Ono, 68, works at his desk at the far end of the room and tells her, without looking up, that staring at it won't make
it go any faster. "It might," she says. The liquid turns blue. She runs the length of the lab and sets the flask down
in front of him. He looks at it, then at her: "...Fine. It might."

Quiet and dry, then one fast beat when the colour turns, then quiet again. English voices.
