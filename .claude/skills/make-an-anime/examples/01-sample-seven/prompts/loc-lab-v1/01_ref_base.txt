Location painting for a slice-of-life anime. {{STYLE}}

The location: a long, narrow old university chemistry lab at 4 a.m., seen in one wide 16:9 view from the side, no people. One long aisle runs from the left of the frame to the right between two rows of black-topped lab benches with steel taps, glassware racks and stacked papers. At the left end, one bench lit by a single warm desk lamp, with a small round glass flask of clear liquid on a metal stand under it. At the right end, under tall windows showing a cold blue pre-dawn sky, a cluttered old wooden desk with a white tea cup and piles of paper. The rest of the room in deep soft blue shadow. Plain unmarked glassware and walls: no text, no labels, no posters, no signs anywhere.
