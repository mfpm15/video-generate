Location painting for a quiet seinen anime film. {{STYLE}}

The location: a vast abandoned ship-breaking scrapyard on a cold northern Japanese coast, at dawn in light falling snow, in one wide 16:9 view, no people, no robots, no vehicles. A broad flat field of untouched snow fills the middle of the frame. Beyond it a low dark shore and a grey-blue sea under a pale overcast sky, with a thin colder band of light on the horizon. On the left and right edges, distant low heaps of rusted scrap, half-cut ship hulls and one leaning dead harbour crane, all softened by snow and distance. Plain unmarked metal: no text, no signs, no numbers, no logos anywhere.
