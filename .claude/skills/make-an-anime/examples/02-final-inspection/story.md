# Final Inspection (最終点検)

The user's script. Target about 24 s, up to 35 s allowed.

A snowy dawn in a scrapyard by the sea. A rusted 40-metre robot, powered down for decades, will be cut up tomorrow. Tetsu, the old mechanic who kept it running for 30 years, climbs up for one last check and shares a thermos of tea with it. He grumbles that it never once thanked him, then walks away. Behind him, something answers. We never see the robot speak, and he never turns around.

1. Wide. Snowy dawn, scrapyard by the sea. The 40-metre robot kneels, head bowed, snow piling on its shoulders and the back of its neck. A tiny figure climbs the ladder fixed to its right knee. Only wind and distant waves.
2. Close on the robot's index finger. A red scrap tag is wired around it. Tetsu's gloved hand brushes the snow off the tag, then rests there for a moment. 「明日、切られる。」 "Tomorrow they cut you up."
3. Medium. Tetsu sits on the open palm, back against the base of the thumb. He pours tea from a thermos into a second small cup and sets it carefully on the metal. Steam rises between them. 「三十年直してやったのに……礼のひとつもなかったな。」 "Thirty years I kept you running… and never once a thank-you."
4. Wide from behind. Tetsu climbs down and walks away across the snow. The robot remains kneeling, head still bowed. Only wind and the soft crunch of his boots.
5. Close on the cup. The surface of the tea ripples once, twice, too regular for wind. Cut to the robot's huge dark eye. A faint amber light flickers on and holds. A low, almost subsonic hum begins under the wind.
6. Tetsu stops, back still to the robot. He gives a short, quiet snort, half laugh, half habit. He walks on without turning. Title appears over the empty snow between them. 「……ああ、どういたしまして。」 "…Yeah, yeah. You're welcome."

Palette: cold blue-grey snow and rust, with two warm notes, the tea steam and the amber eye. The red scrap tag is the only strong accent until the eye lights.

Sound: almost entirely diegetic (wind, distant surf, metal creak, the soft pour of tea, boots in snow). A sparse, low score enters only when the amber light appears and stays under the final line. No heroic swell.

Continuity: the hand in shots 2 and 3 must be the same model and the same lighting.
