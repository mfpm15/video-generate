Location painting for a quiet seinen anime film. {{STYLE}}

The location: the inside of a tiny old Japanese tamagoyaki (rolled omelette) shop at 5 a.m., seen in one wide 16:9 view from near the door, no people. A narrow dark-wood counter runs across the middle of the room from left to right, with one round wooden stool on the near side, at the left. Behind the counter, against the back wall: an old two-burner gas stove with a square copper tamagoyaki pan with a wooden handle on it, one warm pendant lamp hanging over the stove, and simple shelves with bowls, a folded cloth and bamboo chopsticks. On the counter: a small white ceramic salt container with its wooden lid closed, and a plain wooden chopping board. In the back-left corner a rolled-up plain indigo cloth shop curtain (noren) with no markings leans on its wooden pole. On the left wall a sliding door with rain-streaked glass shows the cold blue street outside. Warm amber lamp pool in the centre, cold blue at the window, deep soft shadows in the corners, worn and clean, no text, no signs, no calendars, no labels.
