Location painting for a quiet seinen anime film. {{STYLE}}

The location: a narrow old Tokyo shopping street at 5 a.m. in heavy rain, seen from across the street in one wide 16:9 view, no people, no vehicles. A row of tiny two-storey shops with their metal shutters closed and old corrugated tin awnings over the shopfronts, shining with water. In the centre, one tiny shop with a sliding door whose frosted glass window glows warm amber, the only light on the street, under a dented tin awning with water running off its edge, and an empty wooden pole bracket above the door. Wet dark pavement reflecting the amber window, deep blue night, power lines overhead. Plain unmarked shutters and awnings: no text, no signs, no kanji, no shop names, no posters anywhere.
