Character design sheet for a quiet seinen anime film. {{STYLE}}

The character: KIKU MORI, a 74-year-old Japanese woman, clearly elderly, the owner of a tiny omelette shop. Small and very straight-backed, thin. A lined, dignified old face with real wrinkles around the eyes and mouth, age spots on the cheek, heavy eyelids that are often half-closed, a dry unimpressed mouth. Grey hair pulled back in a tight small bun. Thin round reading glasses pushed up on top of her head, never worn over her eyes. Outfit: a plain indigo cotton work jacket with sleeves tied back, a plain white apron over it, dark trousers, plain straw sandals. Old thin hands with visible knuckles and veins.

Expressions: flat and bored with eyes half-closed, frozen and very still with her eyes open, eyes closed with the smallest smile, dry and looking aside. Layout on a plain light-grey background: full-body front, three-quarter, profile and back views in a row on the left two thirds; on the right, a large front-view head close-up, three expressions and one close-up of an old hand holding chopsticks correctly. Same outfit in every view, relaxed standing pose, whole silhouette inside the frame with margins, consistent scale. No text, labels, name tags, colour swatches, numbers, logos or watermarks; no extra characters; no photoreal rendering.
