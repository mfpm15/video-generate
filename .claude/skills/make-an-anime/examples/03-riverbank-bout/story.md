# One Round at the River

A short shonen fight on a riverbank at sunset. Ren, 20, a calm karate man, and Riku, 18, a cocky Muay Thai fighter, agree to one fight. They trade punches with hard hand-to-hand contact and no superpowers. Ren's straight punch knocks Riku off his feet. Riku, flat on the grass, promises he will be stronger next time. Ren answers that he will be waiting.
