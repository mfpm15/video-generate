# Plan: One Round at the River

## Brief and approval scope

A shonen fight of about 17 s, 24 fps, 16:9, 1080p, requested after a first shonen test (a flame-and-lightning battle keyed on green screen) failed. Every shot is painted H3, built from short cuts, with a partial camera arc and no full rotation. Japanese voices with English subtitles. Prices are not quoted here: check the fal.ai pages of each model before running.

## What changes from the first attempt

Hand-to-hand contact instead of flames and lightning, a real place instead of a floating arena, no auras, cuts instead of a single spinning take, and a story with a beginning and an ending.

## Revision: panels and tracked run

The first cut opened on waist-up green-screen sprites over a painted plate, and both men ran at each other in one shot. User review rejected both: they read as green screen. Replaced by two panel keyframes and H3 takes with the paper cut drawn in p5, and one tracked runner. Authorizes 3 keyframes, 3 H3 clips and 1 SFX, with up to 2 retakes each. Dialogue, score, mix and the fight shots are unchanged.
