The character: RIKU, an 18-year-old Japanese man, a Muay Thai fighter, an adult with adult proportions. Very fair, pale, light Japanese skin with a cool ivory tone on the face and whole body, not tan, not bronzed, not orange. Shorter and wiry with a compact athletic build, a bright, cocky, boyish adult face with large dark-brown eyes and a confident grin, messy spiky black hair with a short undercut, a small plaster on one cheek. Outfit: a plain sleeveless dark-navy training shirt, plain red satin Muay Thai shorts with a white waistband and no writing, red hand wraps on both hands, white ankle wraps, bare feet. Restless, cheeky, hungry presence, always bouncing on his toes.

Expressions: a cocky grin, a fierce fighting shout, an exhausted smile with one eye half closed. Layout on a plain light-grey background: full-body front, three-quarter, profile and back views in a row on the left two thirds; on the right, a large front-view head close-up and three expressions. Same outfit in every view, relaxed standing pose, whole silhouette inside the frame with margins, consistent scale. Pale ivory skin in every view, with soft pink shadows and no tan. No text, labels, name tags, colour swatches, numbers, logos or watermarks; no extra characters; no photoreal rendering.
